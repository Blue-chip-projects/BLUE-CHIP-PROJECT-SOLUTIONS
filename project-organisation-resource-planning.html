Jenasaro / Project Organisation Charts and Resource Planning

JENASARO PLATFORM

See who leads, who owns the area and who delivers the work.

Jenasaro brings the project organisation chart together with teams, resources and area responsibilities. A standard connected tree puts the Project Director or Senior Project Manager above assistants and department heads, with teams and workers beneath their respective departments.

Request a demoExplore features

Explore the whole project or focus on one team

Expand and collapse branches to inspect departments, subcontractors and workers. Zoom and pan across the project, fit the chart to the view, or focus a team in full screen with its own leader at the root. Return to the full project when you need the wider reporting structure.

Open the person behind the role

Select a person to inspect their profile, role in the organisation and recorded contact details. Profile information depends on the records available to your project and your access permissions; the public marketing website does not expose employee contact records.

Keep organisation and resource responsibilities connected

The chart uses project organisation and resource records rather than a separate presentation-only drawing. Authorised users can edit the structure within the organisation function and work with linked teams, area ownership and resource-loader information. Review assignments and missing records as the project scope changes.

Distinguish organisations without losing the reporting lines

Client and main-contractor groups use distinct colours, with different shades for subcontractors. Organisation names and role labels remain visible alongside colour so users can identify the company and reporting relationship.

Share a readable project structure

Print a graphical organisation chart in supported A4, A3, A1 or A0 layouts, or use the detailed register. Choose a project overview or a focused branch for the audience. Confirm the selected view and print preview before distributing contact information.

Learn the connected workflow

Use Academy practice to explore organisation navigation, person profiles, resource planning and area responsibilities. Ask for a demo showing how your department and subcontractor structure would be represented.
