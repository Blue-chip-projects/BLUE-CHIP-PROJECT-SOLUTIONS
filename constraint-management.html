Constraint management as make-ready control

Constraints can include design information, materials, access, permits, predecessor work, labour, equipment, approvals or other conditions required before an activity can be executed. Capturing them is only the first step. Effective make-ready planning also needs ownership, required dates, status and visibility against the affected work.

Jenasaro is designed to keep those elements together rather than maintaining a standalone constraint register disconnected from the programme.

Link blockers to the work they affect

Activity context

Connect the constraint to the activity or production task that cannot proceed.

Ownership

Make the responsible person or party visible so release actions have clear accountability.

Required dates

Track when the blocker must be cleared relative to planned production.

Escalation

Surface ageing or critical constraints in management views and project reporting.

Use constraint trends to improve planning

Repeated blockers often reveal systemic issues in design release, procurement, access planning or decision-making. Because Jenasaro retains constraint categories and production context, teams can analyse recurring causes rather than treating every blocked task as an isolated event.
