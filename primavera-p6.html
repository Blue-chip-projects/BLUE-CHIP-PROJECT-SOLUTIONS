Keep P6 as programme context while adding a production layer

Primavera P6 is widely used for programme scheduling, logic and milestone control. Daily construction production, however, usually requires additional information about areas, workfront readiness, constraints, teams and short-cycle commitments. Jenasaro is designed to bridge that gap.

Imported programme information can be organised into the Jenasaro project structure and then used as context for production planning rather than requiring planners to rebuild every activity manually.

What the production layer adds

Make-ready planning

Identify blockers before scheduled activities are expected to start.

Daily commitments

Turn programme activities into executable work that field teams can commit and complete.

Team and area context

Connect schedule activities to subcontractors, crews, owners and physical project locations.

Performance feedback

Use PPC, reasons, constraint trends and milestone movement to improve control.

Avoid separate versions of the plan

The practical objective is a traceable connection between higher-level schedule intent and the production decisions made closer to execution. That gives planners and delivery teams a shared reference while still allowing each discipline to work at the level of detail it needs.
