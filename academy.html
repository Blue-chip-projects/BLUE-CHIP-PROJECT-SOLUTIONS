JENASARO PLATFORM

Learn the workflow. Practise the features. Keep your progress.

Jenasaro Academy helps project teams learn how the software supports planning and production control. Guided exercises and feature-specific practice complement the public learning guides, with access through the Jenasaro platform.

Request a demoExplore features

Training that follows the software

Explore practice for organisation charts and focused team views, person profiles, resource rosters and rates, area responsibilities, permissions, baseline and forecast workflows, drawings, reporting, exports and update notices. Feature access remains subject to your account and project permissions.

Keep completed work complete

Unchanged training sections retain their completion status. New feature sections track progress separately, so a product update does not require learners to repeat every completed section. New sections are shown as not yet completed until the learner works through them.

Review what changed

In-app update notices show the release number and a summary of changes, with a route to Academy training and review. Users can dismiss the notice or choose to be reminded later.

Practise before applying the workflow

Use guided training to understand the controls and expected decisions before making changes in a live project. Supported tablet and offline workflows include their own limits and reconnect/conflict-review steps; not every platform operation is available offline.

Open the Academy through your platform account

Sign in to Jenasaro and open the Academy from the platform. If you are evaluating the software, request a demonstration of the training experience. For general planning concepts without a platform account, start with the public learning guides.
