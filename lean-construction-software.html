What Lean construction software should connect

Lean production control is not one meeting or one chart. It is a control system that links what should happen, what can happen, what teams commit to do, what was actually completed, and what the project learns from the difference. Jenasaro is designed around that loop.

Make work ready

Identify constraints, responsible owners and release dates before work reaches the field.

Commit executable work

Move ready activities into daily and short-term production plans with clear ownership.

Measure reliability

Use PPC and reason data to understand whether planning promises are being kept.

Re-plan from evidence

Feed actual production status, delays and constraints back into future planning decisions.

From master programme to production plan

Traditional project schedules are essential for contractual dates, logic and milestone forecasting, but they are often too abstract for daily production control. Jenasaro keeps the baseline and schedule relationships visible while allowing planners and construction teams to work at the level of areas, crews, workfronts and daily commitments.

This makes it possible to retain the discipline of a logic-linked programme while creating a more operational layer for short-term planning and execution.

Production information for planners, managers and field teams

Different project roles need different views of the same work. Planners may need logic, predecessors and milestone movement. Area owners need ready work, constraints and team commitments. Management needs trends, exceptions and forecast movement. Jenasaro is designed to keep those views connected instead of recreating the same information in separate files.

  • Look-ahead and make-ready planning
  • Daily commitments and completion tracking
  • Constraint ownership and escalation
  • PPC and reasons for non-completion
  • Takt and workfront planning
  • Team capacity and subcontractor visibility
