CONSTRUCTION SCHEDULING SOFTWARE

Construction scheduling that stays connected to production in the field.

Jenasaro combines logic-linked construction scheduling with production planning. Baselines, current dates, milestones and relationships stay visible while teams plan executable work, resolve constraints and commit activities closer to the workface.

Schedule logic without losing operational control

Jenasaro supports common dependency relationships including finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish and start-to-finish, together with lag. Planners can work with baseline and current programme information while maintaining a direct connection to production-control activities.

The objective is not to replace disciplined scheduling with a task list. It is to connect schedule intent to the shorter control cycles used to actually deliver construction work.

Connect schedule dates to crews, areas and constraints

A schedule becomes more useful for production management when it can answer operational questions: Which team is available? Which area is ready? What constraint is blocking release? What work is due today? Jenasaro brings those production variables alongside schedule information so project teams can make decisions with context.

For projects already planned in Primavera P6, Jenasaro also includes a P6 import path so programme information can be brought into the production environment rather than rebuilt from zero.
