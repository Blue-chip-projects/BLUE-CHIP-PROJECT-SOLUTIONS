Jenasaro / Construction Software Features

JENASARO PLATFORM

One connected view of the plan, the people and the work.

Jenasaro is web-based construction planning, production management and execution-control software for project leaders, planners, construction managers and trade teams. Connect programme intent to the people, areas and daily commitments that deliver it.

Plan and control delivery

Build logic-linked schedules, review baseline and current dates, manage constraints and agree daily production commitments. Last Planner, PPC and takt workflows help teams review readiness, reliability and location-based flow.

Construction schedulingProduction management

Connect people, departments and resources

Use a connected project organisation tree to explore leadership, department heads, subcontractors, teams and workers. Review person profiles and responsibilities alongside resource and area planning.

Review the physical workfront and performance

Connect drawings and supported FBX model objects to activities. Review milestones, variance, contractor KPIs and management report packs. Model-linked planning does not require publishing your project data on this public website.
