What PPC tells a project team

Percent Plan Complete is a simple reliability measure: completed planned commitments divided by total planned commitments for the period. The value becomes more useful when it is paired with context — which team made the promise, where the work was planned, why it was missed, and whether the same cause is repeating.

Jenasaro keeps those data points connected so PPC is not only a weekly percentage but a source of operational learning.

Management visibility without rebuilding the data

Production reporting often consumes time because field status is manually re-entered into spreadsheets and presentations. Jenasaro is designed so PPC, constraints, milestone movement, contractor performance and escalation items can feed management reporting from the same production data used by the delivery team.
