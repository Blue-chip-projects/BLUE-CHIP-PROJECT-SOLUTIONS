Production management between the programme and the workface

Master schedules provide the logic and contractual framework for delivery, but day-to-day construction requires a shorter control cycle. Work needs to be made ready, assigned to teams, coordinated by area, checked for constraints and committed close enough to execution that the plan remains credible.

Jenasaro creates that operational layer without disconnecting it from the underlying schedule. Planners, construction managers, area owners and trade teams can work from the same project information at different levels of detail.

What the production workflow connects

Programme to look-ahead

Bring schedule activities, logic, baseline dates and milestones into a shorter planning horizon where readiness can be tested.

Look-ahead to commitment

Identify executable work, confirm responsible teams and turn ready activities into daily or short-term commitments.

Commitment to actual

Record what started, what completed and what did not, keeping actual delivery connected to the plan.

Actual to insight

Use PPC, reasons, constraints, milestone movement and team KPIs to improve the next planning cycle and management reporting.

Coordinate work readiness, resources and areas

Execution depends on more than dates. Jenasaro is designed to keep constraints, responsible parties, workfronts, crews, calendars and production capacity visible alongside activities. Teams can see where work is ready, where a blocker remains, and where the same resource has already been committed elsewhere.

  • Daily and short-term production planning
  • Constraint ownership and release tracking
  • Subcontractor, team and capacity planning
  • Area-owner responsibility and filtering
  • Drawing-based workfront planning
  • Milestone and schedule variance visibility
  • PPC and reasons for non-completion

Established methods, practical language

Jenasaro supports Lean construction methods such as Last Planner System, make-ready planning, PPC and takt planning. These methods sit within the broader production-management workflow rather than requiring every user to understand Lean terminology before the process becomes useful.
