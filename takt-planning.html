Make flow visible

Takt planning focuses attention on production rhythm, locations and trade sequence rather than viewing activities only as isolated bars on a timeline. Jenasaro provides planning surfaces for structuring work by area and process so teams can see how production is intended to move through the project.

This is particularly useful for repetitive or location-based work where reliable handovers between teams matter as much as individual activity durations.

Connect takt thinking to resource and schedule information

Areas and workfronts

Organise work around project locations and the physical fronts that crews must access.

Trade sequence

Represent the intended order of operations and make downstream handovers clearer.

Team loading

Check where crews are already committed so takt plans do not assume the same team is in two places.

Schedule context

Keep takt decisions connected to milestones and wider programme requirements.

Use drawings as a production-planning surface

Jenasaro also supports workfront planning on drawings. Teams can use layout information to communicate production zones, dates, activities and workfronts during planning sessions. This helps connect abstract sequence discussions to the physical space in which the work will be executed.
