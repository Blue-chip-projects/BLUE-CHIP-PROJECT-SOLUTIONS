Execution control is the connection between plan and actual

A schedule can show when work should happen. Execution control adds the operational questions required to deliver it: Is the area ready? Is the team available? Are constraints cleared? Has the activity been committed? Did it start? Did it complete? What changed, and what does that mean for the next milestone?

Jenasaro keeps those questions tied to the same activities, locations, teams and milestones so the project can manage delivery without rebuilding the story in separate trackers.

Control the work at the right level

Work readiness

Identify constraints and release conditions before activities are committed to the field.

Daily commitments

Commit executable work by area and responsible team, then track completion against the agreed plan.

Planned vs actual

Keep baseline intent visible while current dates and actual delivery show where work is early, on plan or moving late.

Escalation

Surface unresolved constraints, milestone movement, performance trends and management flags from the live production workflow.

From site information to management project performance analytics

Jenasaro is designed to turn execution data into management-ready information rather than treating reporting as a separate exercise. PPC, team KPIs, constraint status, milestone trends and progress evidence can be drawn from the same workflow used to plan and control the work.

  • Actual starts and completions
  • Daily commitment status
  • PPC and reasons for non-completion
  • Milestone trend and schedule movement
  • Constraint and escalation visibility
  • Team and subcontractor performance
  • Resource and workfront coordination

Designed for planners, construction managers and project leadership

Different roles need different levels of detail, but they should not need different versions of the truth. Jenasaro connects schedule logic for planners, ready work and commitments for area owners, field-focused task views for construction managers, and performance trends for management.
